# Dosique — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026 — NOTE: replace this line with your contact
email and a public URL before publishing on Google Play._

Dosique is a medication reminder app that works entirely on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Dosique has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads and
no server. The developer cannot see your data.

## What is stored on your device

The medicines, schedules, dose history, appointments, photos you add, and
settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your
phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Sharing

Data leaves your device only when **you** choose to share it: the share
sheet, WhatsApp/SMS summaries, the doctor-report PDF, or a JSON backup
file you export yourself.

## Permissions

- **Notifications / exact alarms** — to remind you at dose time.
- **Boot completed** — to restore reminders after the phone restarts.
- **Photo picker** — only when you choose to attach a photo of a medicine.
  Dosique uses the system photo picker and does not access your whole
  photo library.

## Health disclaimer

Dosique is a reminder and recording tool only. It is not a medical device,
does not provide medical advice, and does not check drug interactions.
