FULL DESCRIPTION (2665 / 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Never miss a dose again — and never pay a subscription to be reminded.

Dosique is a calm, private medication reminder for you and the people you care for. Add your medicines once, and Dosique reminds you at the right time, keeps a clear record of every dose, and warns you before you run out. It is completely free: no ads, no paywall, no account, no subscription — every feature is unlocked from the start.

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOUR ROUTINE
• On-time reminders with your choice of 5 gentle tones
• Snooze for 10, 20 or 30 minutes when a dose catches you busy
• Mark Taken, or Skip with a reason, in one tap
• Separate profiles for you, family members and pets — unlimited medicines for everyone

EVERY SCHEDULE TYPE
• Every day, at one or more times
• Specific weekdays
• Every X days
• Every X hours within a time window
• As needed (PRN) — no reminder, just a quick log when you take it
• Courses with a fixed length, like a 7-day antibiotic course, with Day X of Y progress that stops by itself

HISTORY YOU CAN FIX
• A month calendar that shows how each day went
• Made a mistake? Edit any past dose — mark it taken, change the time it was taken, or clear the mark
• Streaks, plus adherence for this week, the last 30 days, and each medicine

STOCK & REFILLS
• Dosique counts your tablets, capsules, ml, drops or puffs as you take them
• Low-stock warnings before you run out, plus a weekly refill summary on a day you choose
• Expiry-date alerts 30 days ahead — and a clear flag once a medicine has expired

FOR FAMILY & DOCTORS
• Share today's schedule or a missed-dose summary by WhatsApp, SMS or any app — you choose exactly what to send; there is no live tracking and no shared account
• A tidy doctor report (PDF) with your medicine list, 30-day adherence and dose history for a date range you pick
• Appointment reminders for doctor and pharmacy visits

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads — the app does not even ask for internet access
• Everything — medicines, dose history, photos you add, backups — stays on your phone
• Data only leaves your device when you choose to share or export it yourself
• Optional large-text senior mode, dark theme, and a photo for each medicine if you want one
• Full JSON backup and restore, so your data moves with you to a new phone

IMPORTANT
Dosique is a reminder and recording tool only. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not check drug interactions. Always follow your prescriber's instructions, and talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your medicines.
